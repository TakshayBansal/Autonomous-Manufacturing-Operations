# GenuineGigs — Manufacturing Operations Layer

GenuineGigs sits on top of your existing ERP—such as SAP or Oracle—and helps teams complete the operational work that happens around ERP transactions. The ERP remains the system of record. GenuineGigs becomes the shared layer for work, coordination, documents, decisions, approvals, and exceptions.

## Streamline procurement from requirement to receipt

Procurement work is often spread across ERP screens, spreadsheets, email, supplier documents, calls, and individual follow-ups. GenuineGigs connects this work into one guided flow:

**Material requirement → RFQ → Supplier quotations → Comparison → Approval → Purchase order → Gate entry → Store receipt → Quality inspection**

Every stage shows the responsible person, next action, due date, supporting evidence, and current blocker. Supplier quotations and documents remain linked to the transaction. Approvals and exceptions stay visible instead of disappearing into inboxes. When an action must be posted or communicated externally, GenuineGigs uses controlled integration with the ERP and other systems.

This gives procurement teams one place to prepare supplier requests, track responses, verify quotation data, compare suppliers, route approvals, prepare purchase orders, and hand the complete context to stores and quality.

## Help every employee work faster with a role agent

Each employee receives an agent designed around their role, responsibilities, permissions, and assigned work. The agent understands the employee’s tasks and connected business records, so it can help with real work rather than operate as a generic chatbot.

Role agents can:

- explain what needs attention and why;
- prepare RFQs, messages, checklists, and follow-ups;
- extract and organize information from supplier quotations;
- highlight missing fields, delivery risks, commercial deviations, and blockers;
- prepare supplier comparisons using verified data;
- summarize decisions and provide the evidence behind them;
- create governed tasks and prepare the next step in the workflow.

The employee remains in control. Agents can prepare and recommend, but consequential actions—such as approving a supplier, issuing a purchase order, posting to the ERP, updating inventory, or closing an exception—remain with the authorized person.

## Use the organization’s hierarchy to reduce coordination overhead

GenuineGigs mirrors the company’s reporting and operating structure. Manager agents can review formal team status, identify overdue or blocked work, and delegate clear objectives through the reporting hierarchy. Employee agents receive the objective with the relevant references, expected outcome, constraints, and due date.

This creates a structured coordination loop:

**Manager delegates → Employee agent helps prepare the work → Progress is updated from the real workflow → Manager sees status, blockers, and outcome**

Managers no longer need to repeatedly ask for updates across calls and messages. Employees receive clearer requests with the context required to act. Delegation, follow-ups, reminders, and escalation remain linked to the underlying task and business record.

## Improve communication between departments

Procurement, stores, quality, plant management, and other teams work from the same connected process while retaining their own responsibilities. Structured handoffs carry the required records and evidence forward—for example, an issued purchase order to gate, received quantity to stores, and inspection requirements to quality.

Agents can help teams request updates and coordinate work without exposing private conversations or allowing one role to act with another role’s authority. Everyone sees the information needed for their responsibility, while decisions, handoffs, and exceptions remain traceable.

## The result

GenuineGigs helps manufacturers reduce manual coordination, prepare procurement work faster, surface delays earlier, improve delegation and follow-through, and maintain a reliable trail of documents and decisions. It adds an intelligent, governed system of work around the ERP—without disrupting the systems that already run the business.
